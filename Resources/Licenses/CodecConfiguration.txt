Ring additional codec configuration

JPEG XL: libjxl (BSD-3-Clause), Highway (Apache-2.0), Brotli (MIT), bundled statically.
AVIF: existing libavif (BSD-2-Clause) and libaom (BSD-2-Clause / patent grant),
with AV1 decoding enabled. Exact notices accompany these libraries.

Video: library-only FFmpeg configured with --enable-gpl, statically linked with
x264 and x265 (GPL version 2 or later), SVT-AV1 (BSD-3-Clause-Clear), and Opus
(BSD-3-Clause). FFmpeg's resulting configuration is GPL version 2 or later;
it must not be described or distributed as an LGPL-only build. Distribution
of the combined application must satisfy the applicable GPL source and license
obligations, or use separately obtained compatible commercial codec licenses.
No external FFmpeg executable, Homebrew installation, or codec download is
required at runtime. Runtime capability checks reflect the linked encoders.

Reproducible source versions and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in
Vendor/modern-image-dependencies.lock.json and Vendor/video-dependencies.lock.json.
Local libavif animation-transform and FFmpeg timestamp patches are retained in
Vendor/patches and Tools/patches respectively. The accompanying build scripts
reproduce the static libraries. These changes have not been released.
